Distance-d Colouring Game

Before you start

Two players, one colour at a time

  1. On your turn, click an uncoloured circle, then click a colour.
  2. A colour is only allowed if no vertex within distance d is already using it. Blocked colours grey out once you pick a vertex.
  3. Alice wins if every vertex ends up coloured. Bob wins the moment one uncoloured vertex has no colour left.
  4. Alice always moves first. You can play either side.

Some levels cannot be won from your side — that is deliberate, and the page will tell you before you start. Losing those is the lesson, not a mistake.

Which side?

Alice wants every vertex coloured. Bob wants one vertex left with no legal colour. Alice always moves first.

How to play

Two players take turns. On your turn, pick an uncoloured vertex and give it a colour that no vertex within distance d is already using.

Alice wins if every vertex ends up coloured. Bob wins the moment some uncoloured vertex has no legal colour left. Alice moves first.

At d = 1 only touching vertices must differ. At d = 2 anything within two steps must differ, and so on. Larger d is always harder.

Dashed grey lines show pairs that are not joined in the graph but still block each other at this distance.
